    What happens in a review

    1. Masking. Client names, SSNs, account numbers, contact details and personal balances are replaced with tokens before any text leaves the server.
    2. Inspection. Each section is checked against the active SEC/FINRA rules. A finding is kept only if its quote appears word for word in the document.
    3. Disclosures. Required disclosures for the document type are matched by meaning, not keywords, and missing ones are listed.
    4. Decision. You approve or reject. The AI can flag issues but cannot decide.
